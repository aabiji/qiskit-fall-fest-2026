This repository contains our solution to the agriculture challenge at the
[Qiskit Fall Fest 2026](https://qiskit-fall-fest-2026-quanta-usask.github.io/)
quantum computing hackathon at the University of Saskatchewan that ran from November 25–27, 2026.
Our team consists of Varshika Sasidharan and Abigail Adegbiji, and **we won the agriculture track!**
